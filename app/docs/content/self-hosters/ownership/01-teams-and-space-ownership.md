summary: Who owns a Space, who can be let into it, and what only an owner can do.
---

Who owns a Space is a separate question from who is signed in. A Space belongs
either to a person or to an organization. Sharing lets other people in. Transfer
changes who it belongs to.

## People and organizations

A Space owned by a person belongs to that account. A Space owned by an
organization belongs to the organization, and organization members hold a role
in it: owner or member. Owners manage the membership, the invitations, the
billing, and the organization's Spaces.

## Sharing a Space

Invite people to a Space, see who is in it, remove someone, or cancel an
invitation that has not been taken up. An invitation addressed to a specific
username still has to be accepted, and depending on the settings may need an
owner to approve it as well.

## Transferring a Space

The transfer dialog moves a Space between a person and an organization. It
changes who owns the Space. It does not copy anything that is sitting in a
browser on a particular machine; moving that is a separate transfer.

## What only an owner can do

When an organization's records report a problem, an owner can repair them, and
if repair is not enough, reinitialize them.

Reinitialize is destructive. It rewrites the organization's records in place,
and there is no undo. Only an owner can run it, and nobody else has a recovery
path of their own if things go wrong.
