A Space is a container for one project in Spacewave. Spaces you create without
an account stay on the device that made them. You can move them to Spacewave
Cloud, Spacewave's hosted service, to get sync, backup, and access from your
other devices. Nothing moves until you choose to move it.

## Sign in first

Create or sign in to a Spacewave Cloud account. Cloud gives you encrypted
storage, sync, backup, shared Spaces, and access from your other devices.

If you already have work on this device, Spacewave shows **You have local
data** and asks what to do with it:

- **Migrate my data to Cloud** opens the transfer page, where you move your
  Spaces into Cloud.
- **Keep sessions separate** leaves your local work where it is. You can use
  the local account and the Cloud account side by side.

## Move the Spaces you want

The transfer page lists what is on this device and lets you choose which
Spaces to move. Start the transfer and watch its progress. You can cancel it.
If a transfer stops partway, for example because the app closed, it resumes
where it stopped the next time you open the page.

Move everything you want to keep. A backup key is not a copy of your work. It
only gets you back into your account.

## Then link your other devices

Once your Spaces are in Cloud, [link your other
devices](/docs/users/devices/link-devices) with a pairing code. They all see
the same Spaces. In settings you can remove access for a device you no longer
use.

## If you cancel a paid plan

You keep full access until the end of the billing period you paid for. After
that, your Cloud data stays readable for 30 days, so you can export it or
subscribe again.
