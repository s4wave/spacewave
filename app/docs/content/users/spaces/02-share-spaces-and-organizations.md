A Space is a container for your work in Spacewave. You can let other people
into a Space, and you can give a Space to an organization. An organization is a
group account for a team. Letting people in and changing who a Space belongs to
are separate actions.

## Who a Space belongs to

A Space belongs either to one person's account or to an organization. Each
member of an organization has one of two roles: owner or member. Owners manage
the members, the invitations, the billing, and the organization's Spaces.

## Invite people to a Space

From a Space you can invite people, see who has access, remove someone, and
cancel an invitation that nobody has accepted yet.

An invitation sent to a specific username still has to be accepted by that
person. Depending on the Space's settings, an owner may also need to approve it
before the person can open the Space.

## Move a Space to or from an organization

The transfer dialog changes who a Space belongs to. You can move a Space from
your own account to an organization, or back again.

A transfer changes ownership only. It does not copy work that is stored only in
a browser on one machine. Moving that work is a separate step, described in
[Move to Cloud](/docs/users/accounts/move-to-cloud).

## When a Space will not open

If a Space cannot be opened, Spacewave shows what went wrong and offers two
actions:

- **Repair** tries to fix the Space. Check the Space's current state before you
  choose it.
- **Reinitialize** rewrites the broken Space in place, keeping its ID and
  address. It cannot be undone.

Only the Space's owner can run either action. For a Space that belongs to an
organization, only an organization owner can run them. Other people see the
actions but cannot use them.
